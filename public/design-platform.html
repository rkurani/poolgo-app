Same brand, two registers

Pure speaks to homeowners — plain-spoken, warm, leads with the customer's pool. Platform speaks to operators and developers — dispassionate, precise, leads with the metric or the API.

Discipline

Don'ts — what keeps Pure and Platform from blurring

Don't (on Platform)

  • Use pool photography. Network and data graphics only.
  • Use Pure's loose 16px body. 14px and tabular.
  • Use the OEM color cascade (data-brand). That's a Pure cascade only — it's how the consumer side reads provenance per card.
  • Talk to the homeowner. Every word on Platform is addressed to operators or developers.

Do (on Platform)

  • Use the status palette. Every state has one of four meanings.
  • Lean on tables, dense lists, and stat strips. Density = trust.
  • Use the network diagram convention (Hardware → PoolGo → Surfaces). Repeat it across surfaces so it becomes a brand asset.
  • Show real numbers. "62 partners on the roadmap" beats "growing ecosystem."
